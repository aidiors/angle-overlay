# Angle Overlay

[Русский](README.ru.md)

Move your mouse over a line to see it extended across the screen.
The overlay stays on top of your windows and lets clicks pass through.

![Angle Overlay settings window](assets/screenshot.png)

## Get started

Download the Windows x64 ZIP from **Releases → Assets**, extract the entire folder,
and open **AngleOverlay.exe**. Choose your screen and click **Start overlay**.
You do not need Python or a CUDA Toolkit.

- NVIDIA RTX, AMD/Intel through DirectML, and CPU inference.
- Multiple monitors, mixed Windows scales, and portrait screens.
- **Ctrl+Alt+O** to toggle; **Ctrl+Alt+P** to pause; **Ctrl+Alt+Q** to stop.
- Close or minimize settings to the tray; use its menu to exit.
- Images are processed locally without saving or uploading screenshots.
